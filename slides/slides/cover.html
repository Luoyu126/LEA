Are there different timings between the address modes of equivalent x86 instructions?

Adding zero to an LEA can double its latency

Abby Xiong

abbyx@andrew.cmu.edu

Yunyi Chen

yunyic@andrew.cmu.edu

Our question: can two x86 instructions that do exactly the same thing take different time, only because the address is written differently? We found a case where adding a zero displacement doubles the latency, but only on some cores.
