On AI use. Claude drafted the original experiment plan, wrote the benchmark harness — the kernel generator, the timing driver and the figures — added the compiler-output experiment and the VTune micro-op runs, and drafted the write-ups and these slides. Where we went further: it explained our result as "three address parts are slow", and we did not accept that. We insisted on testing index plus displacement on its own, and the two-part form lea rax,[rax*1+0] is still two cycles, which gave the real rule: index and displacement together. We also caught two errors. Its plan said to calibrate with add rax,1 as a one-cycle reference; on these cores that costs about 0.17 cycles because the renamer folds immediate adds, so every number came out five to six times too high, and a second sanity check on imul caught it. And in the extension write-up it turned a 0.25-cycle throughput into seven or eight per cycle, when it is four. Every number on these slides was checked against the raw data.
