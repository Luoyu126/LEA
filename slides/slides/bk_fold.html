Backup, for the 0.17 cycles that shows up on the second chip. On the Raptor Cove P-core, lea rax,[rax+k] and add rax,k behave the same, and both are much faster than one cycle when k is small: about 6 per cycle for k of 0 or 1. Up to 1023 and down to minus 1024 it is still below one cycle, but it costs more as k grows. From 1024 on it is a normal 1-cycle add. Whether the constant is stored in 1 byte or 4 makes no difference; only its value counts. That is the opposite of the E-core effect, which depends on the encoding and not on the value. The Lion Cove P-core on the 285H did not do this for LEA.
