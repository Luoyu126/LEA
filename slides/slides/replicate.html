BACKUP · SECOND CHIP

Does it replicate? A second chip.

On an older E-core (Gracemont, i7-13700H), index + displacement still costs 2 cycles.

Throughput drops 4× on Gracemont (4 per cycle → 1 per cycle), about 2× on Skymont. The quirk existed before Arrow Lake, and was worse.

Bars: i7-13700H, two CPUs per core type. Black ticks: the 285H.

Appendix 1

Backup, if asked whether it is just this one chip. We repeated the whole experiment on a second laptop, an i7-13700H. Its E-core, Gracemont, is one generation older than Skymont. The result is the same: as soon as an index and a displacement are both encoded, the E-core takes 2 cycles, even for plus zero, and even with no base. The P-core takes 1 cycle for all of these. Throughput is hit harder on Gracemont: the fast form runs 4 per cycle, the slow form only 1, a 4 times drop. On Skymont it was about 2 times. So this quirk is not new in Arrow Lake; it was there before, and it was worse.
