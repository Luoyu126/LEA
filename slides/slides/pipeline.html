This sketches the pipelines our hypothesis predicts, cycle by cycle; the test after the method slide checks it. On the P-core, a three-input adder takes base, index and displacement at once: a 3-to-2 compressor reduces three numbers to two, then one add, all in one cycle. On the E-core, the adder takes two inputs per cycle, and the index and displacement compete for the same one. Base plus index is added in cycle 1, then the displacement is added in cycle 2. Because the decoder routes by encoding, a zero displacement still takes the second cycle.
